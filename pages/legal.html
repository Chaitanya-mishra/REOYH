Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2025

At REOYH, we connect students with the right tutors — and doing that means we collect some information about you. We want to be completely open about what we collect, why we need it, and how we keep it safe. This isn't just legal text — it's a commitment we take seriously.

What Information We Collect

  • Basic details — your name, phone number, and email address
  • Location preferences — the area or city where you're looking for tutoring
  • Learning preferences — subjects, grade level, and preferred timing
  • Usage data — how you interact with our platform and features you use
  • Tutor profiles — qualifications, experience, and availability shared by tutors

Why We Collect This Information

  • Matching students with suitable tutors based on preferences and location
  • Sending updates, confirmations, and important notifications
  • Improving our platform based on how people actually use it
  • Resolving issues and responding to support requests

We Do Not Sell Your Data

We will never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing or advertising purposes. Your data stays with us, and we use it only to make REOYH work better for you.

How We Keep Your Data Safe

We take reasonable steps to protect your information from unauthorised access, misuse, or disclosure. This includes secure data storage, limited internal access, and regular reviews of our practices.

Your Rights Over Your Data

  • Request to view the information we hold about you
  • Ask us to update or correct any inaccuracies
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data

Questions about your privacy? Reach out to us at support.reoyh@gmail.com

Refund Policy

Last updated: April 2025

We aim to keep payment processes fair for both students and tutors.

REOYH Is a Platform

REOYH connects students with tutors but does not directly provide tutoring services. Payments are typically handled between users.

No Refunds After Service Begins

Once tutoring sessions have started, refunds are generally not possible, as the tutor has already invested time and effort.

Dispute Handling

In genuine cases (no-show, misconduct, etc.), we review situations individually and aim for fair resolution.

Refund Processing

If approved, refunds are processed within 7–10 business days through the original payment method.

For concerns, contact us at support.reoyh@gmail.com
